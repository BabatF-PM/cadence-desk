Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Cadence Desk ("we", "our", or "us") provides meeting intelligence, automated transcript summarization, and multi-timezone scheduling workflows. This Privacy Policy explains how we access, collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Access and Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in via Google OAuth, we access your basic profile identity (name and email address) to manage your session.
  • Google Drive Files: When you explicitly select a meeting transcript (.docx or .txt) from Google Drive, we read its contents in ephemeral memory solely to extract meeting summaries and action items.
  • Google Calendar Metadata: We evaluate timezone and availability metadata strictly to coordinate multi-participant meeting schedules.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
Cadence Desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. How We Use and Protect Data

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or Google user data.
  • We do not transfer user data to third-party advertising platforms or data brokers.
  • We do not use Google user data to train generalized AI models without explicit, affirmative consent.
  • All transcript processing is ephemeral in runtime memory.

3. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You can revoke Cadence Desk permissions at any time via your Google Account Security Settings. Using the "Log Out" button within the app purges all transient tokens and session cache from your browser storage.
